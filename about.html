About

I design products that make complex things feel effortless — and I build them too, because understanding how something works makes it easier to design it well.

I'm Bhawna, a Digital Product Designer based in India. I've spent the last few years working at the intersection of UX, systems design, and front-end craft — currently helping shape UPI experiences at Zeta for PayZapp.

Before fintech, I designed for healthcare systems, where the stakes were high and clarity wasn't optional. That experience left me with a deep respect for systems thinking and a habit of sweating the details.

When I'm not in Figma, I'm probably vibe coding something, tinkering with GSAP, or arguing about type pairings.
