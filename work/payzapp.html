Driving 79% growth in Wallet-on-UPI adoption

Product: PayZapp by HDFC Bank  |  Timeline: 2024  |  Role: End-to-End Design Lead

Overview

This project focused on increasing the adoption of PayZapp Wallet (Zapp Account) as a payment instrument within UPI transactions.

Despite having a high-potential Wallet (Zapp Account), monthly active usage was stagnant at 3% of all UPI transactions.

As the design owner for UPI, I redefined the problem from a "lack of trust" to a "discovery failure", leading to a contextual redesign that boosted monthly adoption by 79% without increasing payment drop-offs.

My Role

I was the sole design owner for the UPI payment experience at PayZapp. This project involved me driving the problem framing, leading all design decisions, collaborating with a content designer on messaging, negotiating constraints with the bank's team, and advocating for edge case flows that were initially out of scope.

The PM owned prioritisation; I owned the design strategy and its rationale end-to-end.

Business Context

Why PPI Wallet for UPI?

For PayZapp, adding Prepaid Zapp Account as a payment instrument on PayZapp was a strategic necessity to:

  • Reduce bank dependency: PPI bypasses bank-side server downtimes, improving transaction reliability and reducing failure rates outside PayZapp's control.
  • Unlock interchange revenue: A 1.1% interchange fee (similar to MDR) applies to PPI-based UPI transactions exceeding ₹2,000 on merchant payments (P2M). This fee is paid by the merchant — meaning every eligible PPI transaction above that threshold generates revenue that a standard Savings Account UPI transaction does not.
  • Platform revenue share: Zeta earns 10% for every x number of transactions done via Zapp Account.

Discovery

Data Insights

We had GTM for Zapp Account as Prepaid payment instrument a while back, although the usage of wallet for UPI payments was low.

I was asked to take a fresh look. Initially, the team's hypothesis was that low adoption could be due to lack of trust, usability issues, low relevance or lack of awareness.

I initiated a data-deep dive to understand the root cause:

The Root Cause

Discovery Failure,
Not Trust Failure

PayZapp's payment flow showed only the last-used payment method. Since most users had historically paid via Savings Account, the wallet was functionally invisible every single time they paid.

Problem Statement

The Pivot

  • From: Users aren't using the wallet; we might need more awareness.
  • To: How might we intercept the existing payment habit with a contextual discovery moment that converts savings-account users into wallet users without adding friction?

Conceptualisation & Key Decisions

By this point, I was clear that we need to have wallet shown upfront to the users on payment entry page. Considering that, I evaluated three distinct approaches to solve for discovery:

Rejected ❌

1. Make wallet the default

High adoption potential

But

Changing a user's payment method without consent is a trust violation in financial UX. In a product where users move real money, broken trust is a catastrophic cost — one that doesn't show up in week-one metrics but bleeds retention for months.

Rejected ❌

2. Distinct visuals

High Visibility

But

Payment flows are speed-sensitive. Users in the act of paying are focused, not browsing. Adding a promotional layer creates cognitive load at exactly the wrong moment — and banner blindness means it would decay quickly.

Chosen ✅

3. Contextual inline discovery

  • Visible at decision moment
  • No disruption to existing flow
  • Preserved user control

Design principle:

Don't interrupt the habit. Redirect it.

The Solution

UPI transaction via wallet experience

What it does:

  • Always visible at the moment of payment — not hidden behind a scroll or a tab
  • Lets users switch in a single tap, preserving the muscle memory of the existing flow
  • Shows available balance inline, making the decision immediate and informed
  • Adds zero friction to users who choose not to switch

Designing for (not so) Edge Case

Insufficient wallet balance

First-time failure = lost trust.
In fintech, lost trust = lost user.

The most common failure state was predictable and a core case to increase the adoption.

User enters amount

Selects Zapp Account

Oops! not sufficient funds

The Backstory

This particular flow was earlier not in scope when GTM was launched. Hence, a user had to add money separately via Zapp Account homepage which is within PayZapp and then only they can do any payment using Zapp Account.

While working again as an initiative to boost growth on PPI payments, I crafted this flow.

But again due to system level technical constraints, I was told to craft a flow which helps us move fast to release the updates.
